Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does offline mode and data syncing work?

SkyTrails is designed offline-first. Your checklists, photos, and bird sightings are saved on your local device database using Apple's SwiftData framework. Once you connect to the internet, the app seamlessly uploads and synchronizes these records to our secure cloud database powered by Supabase.

Q: Why can't I see nearby hotspots or my current location?

SkyTrails needs location permissions to search for bird sightings and migration maps around you. Please make sure that:

  • Location Services are enabled on your device (iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services).
  • SkyTrails has permission to access your location (iOS Settings > SkyTrails > Location set to "While Using the App").

Q: How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account and clear all synced logs directly inside the app. Open the Profile tab, navigate to Account Settings, and select "Delete Account". Alternatively, you can email us at the address listed above, and we will purge your user records within 5 business days.
