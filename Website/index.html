Deep Dive

A Premium Experience for iPad

SkyTrails is meticulously designed to take full advantage of the larger canvas on iPad, providing a desktop-class birding experience in the field.

Smart Migration Dashboard

Experience birding on a grand scale. Our iPad-optimized dashboard provides a comprehensive view of migration patterns, hot-spots, and sightings, all powered by MapKit's high-resolution rendering.

  • Real-time migration overlays
  • Advanced spatial filtering
  • Desktop-class data visualization

Intelligent Identification Engine

Our logic-driven identification engine is more intuitive than ever. Use the iPad's split-view or full-screen layout to cross-reference morphological markings with stunning accuracy.

  • Multi-step morphological matching
  • High-fidelity species illustrations
  • Confidence ranking algorithm

Universal Data Persistence

With SwiftData and Supabase integration, your watchlists and sightings are synchronized across all your devices. Start logging on your iPhone and analyze in detail on your iPad.

  • Seamless cloud synchronization
  • Secure offline storage
  • Shared collaborative watchlists
