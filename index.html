01 / Home

Know where to look next.

The home dashboard combines your location with migration activity, upcoming species, nearby birding spots, and fresh stories from the birding world.

  • ⌖ Location-aware bird and hotspot discovery
  • ∿ Migration forecasts and activity windows
  • ◎ Maps that turn predictions into a route

02 / Watchlist

Build a watchlist that grows with you.

Plan species to discover, log birds you have observed, create focused collections for trips, and keep useful details such as dates, locations, notes, and photos together.

  • ＋ Curated watchlists
  • ◉ Observed and to-discover tracking
  • ⌁ Smart rules for place, time, and bird traits

03 / Identification

Turn field clues into a confident match.

Choose the clues you remember: location and date, size, shape, and field marks. SkyTrails ranks likely species and keeps completed identifications in your history.

  • ◇ A guided, multi-step identification flow
  • ◐ Visual size, silhouette, and marking choices
  • ✓ Ranked results with confidence scores
